Trailer

Level Up: Game Shop is a first-person tycoon about running a video game store. You start with one tiny room, a few shelves and a till. Fill it with parody consoles and games from every era, and turn it into the biggest game shop in town.

From one room to four floors

Run the shop

Buy licenses on the shop computer, pick what goes on every shelf row, set the prices, open the doors and work the till until you can pay someone else to.

  • Ten eras of gaming, from 8-bit classics and arcade legends to the disc era, the indie boom and VR, with more than 450 parody games, consoles and accessories.
  • New releases every day. Each morning brings new licenses, and picking the hits is how you get ahead.
  • Trade-ins at the counter. Haggle with sellers who can bluff, and keep an eye on your mail for collectors, press and bills. Beware of the possible scams.

Grow your empire

Happy customers raise your popularity, and popularity brings more people through the door. Save up for the next building upgrade and watch the shop grow from one room to four floors, with stairs, an elevator and the whole street looking in through the glass.

  • Seven shop sizes to grow through, each with more room and more to manage.
  • A town that remembers you. Regulars have names, tastes and budgets. They come back, sell you their old games and post what they think of your shop on social media.
  • Play it your way. Casual, Boss or Gamer difficulty, or tune every rule yourself. There is no game over: a loan can always get you back on your feet.

Hire a crew

Cashiers keep the queue moving and restockers keep the shelves full. Every employee has their own stats, learns on the job and levels up.

  • Pay a bonus to get through a tough rush.
  • Train an apprentice. Take a new hire along and let them learn by watching you.

Build it your way

Pick anything up and move it at any time. The shop is yours, from the sign over the door to the last poster on the wall.

  • Over 100 pieces of furniture and decor: brand shelves, glass counters, display tables, posters and more.
  • Shape your shelves as an L, a T, a U, a cross or a four-sided island.
  • Make it yours with your shop's name over the door and dozens of wall, floor and ceiling finishes.
  • Set the mood with a radio or a set of speakers playing the game's original soundtrack.

Open an arcade

Your shop can be a hangout too. Put in arcade cabinets, PC rigs rented by the hour and VR booths, and people will come to play as well as to shop.

  • Sixteen arcade cabinets, each with its own game running on the screen.
  • Tournaments and midnight launches that pack the shop.
  • High scores. When someone is about to beat the record, the whole floor gathers to watch.

Life in the city

When the shop closes, the city keeps going.

  • Your phone does it all: a map, your bank, music, a camera, photos, your shop's ratings and the Y feed.
  • Find a place to live. Start in a small studio and move up. Sleep to skip the night.
  • Dress up at the boutique, or design your own shirt print.
  • Feeling lucky? The Lucky Pixel casino opens at night with blackjack, poker and slots.
  • And a few surprises at the shop across from the boutique.

About the use of generative AI

Generative AI was used very lightly in Level Up: Game Shop, for a couple of textures, sprites and 3D models. Everything else is human-made. Version 1.0 won't come out until every generated asset has been replaced with human-made work.

I do not support the irresponsible use of AI in creative projects.
